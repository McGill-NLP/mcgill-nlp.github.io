We’re pleased to announce Social Sim’26, the 2nd Workshop on Social Simulation with LLMs, taking place at COLM 2026 in San Francisco on October 9. This year’s theme is *Fidelity in Applications*. The workshop also hosts the *Many Worlds* shared task on evaluating LLM social simulations, with [code](https://github.com/sandbox-social/socsim26_sharedtask) and [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ComplexDataLab/socsim26-sharedtask) openly available. For more information, please refer to the [workshop website](https://sites.google.com/view/social-sims-with-llms/).
