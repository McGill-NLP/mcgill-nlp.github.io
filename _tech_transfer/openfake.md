## [OpenFake](https://vicliv.ca/deepfake-detector/)

<div class="clearfix" markdown="1">
[![image-left](/assets/images/tech_transfer/openfake.webp){: .align-left}](https://vicliv.ca/deepfake-detector/)

OpenFake is an open dataset of real and AI-generated images from a wide range of open-source and proprietary generators, designed for training and evaluating AI-image detectors. We use it to develop detectors that recognize realistic AI-generated images, including those shared on social media.
